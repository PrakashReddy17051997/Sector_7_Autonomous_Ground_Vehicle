## 06.11. after field test log_028
1. Pole hit: ramp mode (no LiDAR obstacle handling, no LiDAR stop) was on for the WHOLE ramp leg 2 (33 m).
   Now only while on the slope (pitch > 4 deg off the start level, + 3 s): RouteFollow::onRamp.
   Front ultrasonic < 20 cm in 2 scans -> obstacle handling (not on the slope).
2. No turn at the ramp foot (Mark 1): pivot rose only to 0.45 and did not turn. Now up to 0.50, and after
   4.5 s without turning a SWING turn (inner wheels stop, outer wheels drive).
3. Back-ups blocked: back ultrasonic reads a constant 16 cm (logs 026-028). SafetyGuard back stop now
   only if the LiDAR rear sector also sees something closer than 45 cm.
4. Path deviation (0.12-0.19 m right, log_028): rover crabs 7.6 deg right of its nose. Path pursuit now:
   adaptive look-ahead Ld = 3 s * v (0.6..1.6 m, + 0.6 m * (1 - match score)), crab compensation
   crabDeg = -7.6, cross-track integral (3 deg per m*s, max 6 deg), light low-pass of the pursuit error.
   Web page: route.track = look-ahead, off path (cm), integral.

## 06.11. after field test log_029
1. "turn time-out, driving on" -> rover stood still 110 s, Resume did nothing: DRIVE -> TURN did not restart
   the turn timer, so every TURN step timed out at once -> DRIVE -> TURN ... with 0 output. Fixed: timer
   restarts on every TURN entry; after a time-out no new turn on the spot for 1.5 m.
2. Open area (leg 2): LiDAR match score 0.3-0.5, the path pursuit turned the rover 50 deg off (yaw -84 ->
   -138) while it believed it was on the path. HOLD legs: path steering only with score >= 0.5 and only
   within +-15 deg of the leg's IMU heading ref; otherwise it holds the relative yaw.
3. Hard braking: 6 stops at 0.30 from single false front-ultrasonic echoes (4-22 cm, LiDAR 3-7 m free).
   SafetyGuard: front ultrasonic stops only if the LiDAR agrees (< 55 cm) or it reads close for 300 ms.
4. Leg 1 with the new tracking: off path mostly within +-10 cm (log_028: 12-19 cm right). Turn at the
   ramp foot worked (yaw -7 -> -61 deg).
5. GPS: 0 satellites in logs 025-029 (log_013: 20 satellites, 100 % fix) - module / wiring, not software.
   One-scan LiDAR "where am I" against the 79 route keyframes picked the wrong leg in 4 of 6 tests
   (log_029) -> not safe for automatic leg switching. Zones need GPS working again.
